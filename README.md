## 🌐 Live Demo

**Live Application:** https://careerforge-ai-one.vercel.app/login

---

## 📌 Project Overview

JobPrepAI is an intelligent interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their technical, aptitude, and behavioral interview skills.

The platform leverages Artificial Intelligence to generate interview questions, evaluate responses, provide personalized feedback, and help users prepare effectively for placements and job interviews.

It serves as a virtual interview assistant that helps candidates gain confidence and improve their chances of success.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Provide AI-powered interview preparation.
* Generate personalized interview questions.
* Improve candidate confidence and communication skills.
* Assist students in placement preparation.
* Provide instant feedback and performance analysis.
* Support technical and behavioral interview practice.
